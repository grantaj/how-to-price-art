Behind the number — data & methodology

We use the publicly advertised asking prices of 2D artworks in four Australian gallery catalogues. We select artists whose researched career stage matches yours. We first adjust each observed price to your artwork's surface area using a modest size-scaling rule, then take a median price for each artist. Finally, we take the median across artists so one artist with many works doesn't dominate the result.

The range shows the middle half of these artist-level comparisons. The size adjustment is a modelling assumption, not a fitted market law. Prices are not confirmed transaction amounts; galleries may give discounts, and their listed prices typically include gallery commissions. The samples are small and deliberately selected, so this result is a rough starting point, not a market-wide statistical estimate.

Career stages are researched from publicly available biographies and documented according to NAVA's guidance. Records with unverified stages are excluded from this calculator. The advanced calculator lets you explore the full database, auction transactions and hypothetical demand assumptions.
