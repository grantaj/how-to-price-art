How is the price calculated?

We compare publicly advertised asking prices of 2D artworks in four Australian gallery catalogues. We select artists whose researched career stage matches yours, adjust each price for artwork area, and take a median price for each artist. We then take the median across artists so one prolific artist doesn't dominate.

The range shows the middle half of these artist-level comparisons. The size adjustment is an assumption, not a measured market law. Prices are not confirmed transaction amounts; galleries may negotiate discounts. The dataset is a small, nonrepresentative sample, so this result is a starting point rather than an authoritative valuation.

Career stages are assessed from public biographies using NAVA's guidelines. Unverified stages are excluded.
